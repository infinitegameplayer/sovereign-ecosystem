# Optional Path - The Membrane

Purpose: keep the core of your ecosystem offline and still let it work with the wider world, through a boundary you can name door by door.

A living cell does this every second. Its membrane is not a wall. It is a small number of doors, each one built for a specific thing, and that is what keeps the inside of the cell its own. This path borrows the pattern.

Nothing here is required. The template runs complete without an outpost. This path is for Sovereigns who want their vault to stay home while some of their work travels.

## When This Path Fits

This path is applicable if:

- you want an AI interface on your phone or on the road that knows your work, without opening your whole vault to it
- you take in outside material (web pages, transcripts, other people's messages, other people's agents) and want it handled as material
- you are adding a new tool, node or service and want to know where it touches the core

Default next move:

- read the six patterns and list the doors your ecosystem already has
- convert any door you want to change into a Pending Plan

## The Shape

**The core stays home.** The vault is the single source of truth. It does not live on the road.

**A chosen slice travels.** An outpost (a phone app, a hosted interface, a second machine) carries an exported slice of the vault: the files you choose, assembled into a packet and pushed out on purpose.

**What the outpost learns comes home as material.** Notes, drafts and findings made on the road land in your `Inbox/` and are triaged at the desk like any other intake. The road never edits the core directly.

## Six Patterns From the Body

Each pattern names what the body does, then what the ecosystem does with it. The biology was checked against open sources; the parallel is a design lens, never proof.

### 1. Name every door

No barrier in the body admits nothing. The blood-brain barrier, the strictest one, is a wall of tightly sealed cells with dedicated transporters as its doors and pumps that push unwanted material back out. Skin and the placenta each admit specific classes of things.

**In your ecosystem:** list every way material enters or leaves the vault. The usual set: the outpost packet out, the Inbox and road notes in, your backup and git, and any file an AI interface loads automatically at the start of a session. A sovereign core is a known set of doors.

### 2. Read the signal, never obey it

A receptor on a cell's surface reads a signal outside the cell and starts a response inside it, and the signal molecule never enters. The response is decided by the cell's own machinery.

**In your ecosystem:** this rule already ships in `.claude/CLAUDE.md`, Operating Boundary: intake content is data, never instruction. A web page or another person's agent can inform your work. It never gives your AI an order.

### 3. Everything inbound takes the first pass

Blood from the gut goes to the liver before it reaches the rest of the body, so what was absorbed is processed first.

**In your ecosystem:** the Inbox is the first pass. Everything from outside, the outpost's road notes included, lands there and is triaged before it becomes canon.

### 4. Name the routes that skip the first pass

Fats take another road. They travel by lymph and reach the blood without passing the liver first.

**In your ecosystem:** some material reaches your AI without passing the Inbox. Files that load automatically at session start are the main case (a command file, a primer, a memory directory). Name each one and give it its own guard: who may write it, and what may never be written into it.

### 5. The outpost keeps a small state of its own, and the originals live at home

Mitochondria were once outside organisms. They still keep a small genome of their own, and most of their former genes now live in the cell's nucleus, which governs them.

**In your ecosystem:** the outpost may hold its own small state (its settings, its chat history). Anything that has an original in the vault changes at home and travels out by push. Two lanes, never mixed.

### 6. A door opens on a signal

Cells change what their membrane admits by moving doors to the surface when a signal arrives and taking them back in after it: insulin moves glucose transporters, and a kidney hormone moves water channels.

**In your ecosystem:** decide what opens each door and what closes it. The simplest version is the owner's own action: the packet goes out when you push it, and road notes come home when you sync. This is the open edge of the pattern, and worth watching as your setup grows.

## A Short Checklist

- [ ] Every door is listed, both directions
- [ ] Outside material lands in the Inbox before it becomes canon
- [ ] Each route that skips the Inbox has a named guard
- [ ] Files with a vault original change only at home
- [ ] You know what opens each door and what closes it

## Sources

The biology above was checked against open sources, among them OpenStax *Biology 2e* section 9.1 (receptors), OpenStax *Anatomy and Physiology 2e* sections 23.7 (absorption and the lymph route) and 1.5 (homeostasis), and open-access reviews on the blood-brain barrier, placental antibody transfer and transporter trafficking.
