## What This Is

The Living Axis of Sovereignty is a three-doctrine framework describing what a principled, durable personal operating system looks like from the inside out. The Ṣāḍguṇya Codex and Ambassador Doctrine (v1.7.0) covered the external engagement layer. This update completes the trifecta.

Three new protocols + one new codex + one meta-governance pattern. Together they answer: WHO must develop (Ṣaṭsthala), WHAT must be in place (Svarāja), HOW sovereignty moves (Ṣāḍguṇya), and HOW governance standards travel forward as the ecosystem grows (Propagation Triad).

## What It Adds

**Living Axis of Sovereignty** (`Council Chamber/Codices/Sovereignty/Living Axis of Sovereignty.md`):

The umbrella codex. Holds the three-doctrine system as a whole. Explains the recursive logic: strategic challenges reveal structural gaps; structural gaps point toward inner development needs; inner development strengthens both structure and strategy. Includes operational coverage table, practical applications (AI systems, communities, personal sovereignty) and the philosophical synthesis. New `Council Chamber/Codices/Sovereignty/` subdirectory.

**Svarāja Doctrine** (`Council Chamber/Protocols/Governance/Svarāja Doctrine.md`):

The structural governance protocol. Six pillars:
- **Svarāja (Self-Rule):** Constitutional autonomy, tool independence. Maps to Constitution, Operating Charter, Interface Adapter Registry.
- **Sūraj (Ethical Governance):** Consent doctrine, approval gates. Maps to Plan → Consent → Execute architecture and pre-tool hooks.
- **Svadeśa (Economic Sovereignty):** Resource independence. Maps to Operations folder and economic infrastructure.
- **Śāstra (Strategic Defense):** Security, knowledge protection, tech sovereignty. Maps to Security Check, Sovereign Tech Watch, Release Hygiene.
- **Saṃskṛti (Cultural Intelligence):** Codices, expression tradition, living narrative. Maps to Codices Index, Scriptorium, Library.
- **Samāj (Collective Intelligence):** Collaboration architecture, alliance building. Maps to Collaborations container and Ambassador Doctrine.

Includes a structural coverage map, maturity assessments for each pillar and a gap assessment. The doctrine does not build new structure — it names and evaluates what already exists.

**Ṣaṭsthala Doctrine** (`Council Chamber/Protocols/Governance/Ṣaṭsthala Doctrine.md`):

The inner mastery protocol. Six stages of personal development:
- **Bhakta Sthala** — Initiation and Commitment
- **Maheśa Sthala** — Embodiment of Power
- **Prāṇaliṅga Sthala** — Flow State and Energy Mastery
- **Śaraṇa Sthala** — Purposeful Contribution
- **Aikya Sthala** — Unified Intelligence Field
- **Siddha Sthala** — Full Mastery and Liberation

Includes a Stage Mirror (reflective table for locating your active working edge), Inner Mastery Flywheel (five-step Stage Sense → Practice Choice → Activation → Story Capture → Reassessment loop), archetypal development mapping (Conduction Codex archetypes mapped to stages), and the Recursive Loop explanation. Designed to load during coaching sessions, North Star recalibrations and quest activations.

**Propagation Triad** (`Council Chamber/Protocols/Governance/Propagation Triad.md`):

A reusable meta-governance design pattern. Three layers: (1) the canonical standard document includes a Propagation Rules section defining what creation surfaces require it; (2) creation skills include an explicit gate enforcing the standard at creation time; (3) CLAUDE.md includes a standing rule applying it at session load and to runtime sub-agents. Ambassador Doctrine is the first instance of all three layers applied together. Apply this pattern whenever a new ecosystem-wide governance standard is embedded.

**Codices Index — Sovereignty section:**

New section added pointing to the Living Axis codex as the philosophical anchor for all three doctrine protocols.

## How to Implement

1. Create `Council Chamber/Codices/Sovereignty/` directory in your vault.
2. Copy `Council Chamber/Codices/Sovereignty/Living Axis of Sovereignty.md` into your vault.
3. Copy `Council Chamber/Protocols/Governance/Svarāja Doctrine.md` into your vault.
4. Copy `Council Chamber/Protocols/Governance/Ṣaṭsthala Doctrine.md` into your vault.
5. Copy `Council Chamber/Protocols/Governance/Propagation Triad.md` into your vault.
6. Add the Sovereignty section to your `Council Chamber/Codices/Codices Index.md`.
7. Optional: add a trigger phrase note to your `.claude/CLAUDE.md` for both Svarāja Doctrine and Ṣaṭsthala Doctrine if you want your AI interface to load them automatically in relevant sessions.

## Prerequisites

None required. The three doctrine protocols stand alone. The Ambassador Doctrine (v1.7.0) and Ṣāḍguṇya Codex (v1.7.0) complete the trifecta, but the Living Axis codex, Svarāja Doctrine and Ṣaṭsthala Doctrine each function independently.

## Optional?

No. The Living Axis is the philosophical foundation for how a sovereign operating system understands itself: its inner development, its structural architecture and its external engagement as a unified recursive system. Svarāja and Ṣaṭsthala name what the ecosystem is already building. The Propagation Triad ensures that future governance standards propagate forward rather than becoming point-in-time rules.
