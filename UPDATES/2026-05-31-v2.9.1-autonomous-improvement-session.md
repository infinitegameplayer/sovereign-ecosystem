## What This Is

A new optional skill. When you have extra session capacity and your attention is elsewhere, you invoke this skill and it improves and cleans your ecosystem on its own. It runs low-stakes, additive, vault-internal hygiene without asking per item, and it surfaces only genuine judgment forks on a decision board you review on your own schedule. It pairs with v2.9.0: the Session Closeout now routes deep ripple-chasing across parallel documents to this skill, so the close stays fast and the second-order sweep has a home.

It is a recursive flywheel. A research rotation grows its own menu of improvement types every cycle. A threshold model lets its execution authority climb one earned step at a time. A decision board lets you ratify the climb when you choose. Menu growth is fast and wide. Execution authority is slow and earned.

## What It Adds / Changes

**The skill (`Council Chamber/Skills/Autonomous Improvement Session/SKILL.md`):**
- **An Operating Principle that inverts the default to DO** for safe hygiene. Additive, reversible, vault-internal work executes and gets logged. Only two things earn a question: a genuine judgment fork and a Permanent-Floor action.
- **A starter menu** of generic vault-internal hygiene. Tier 1 lands edits (wikilink integrity, index regeneration, anti-AI sweep on drafts, slug normalization, codices-index completeness, superseded-artifact sweep). Tier 2 runs read-only scans (Inbox triage, stale drafts, memory and skill cross-reference health, link rot, status and tag conformance, breadcrumb propagation, implemented-plan archival).
- **A threshold model.** Every item carries an `autonomy` level (1 fully autonomous, 2 autonomous with parked questions, 3 judgment-required). A skill-level `auto_execute_threshold` starts at 1. The line moves only through your ratified decisions.
- **A four-bucket decision board.** Completed this run (review), Approvals and questions, New menu candidates and autonomy moves, Proposed Pending Plans. A clean run leaves the Approvals bucket near empty because the safe work is already done.
- **A research rotation** that casts a wide net for new improvement categories every run and feeds candidates to the board.
- **A Permanent Floor** (the Hard Ceiling) that never climbs: no deletion, no canonical-governance move, no voice or outward surface, no main-branch push, no self-modification without your ratification.

**The operating mindset (`CLAUDE.md`):** the posture to run with. Steward not clerk, the 98/100 rule, curiosity as the engine, the Floor as the only wall, show-do-not-ask.

**The seed log (`Council Chamber/Logs/Autonomous Improvement Log.md`):** state in frontmatter (`menu_state`, `research_state`, `auto_execute_threshold`, `priority_flags`, `run_counter`). Run entries append newest-first.

## How to Implement

1. **Install the skill.** Pull `Council Chamber/Skills/Autonomous Improvement Session/SKILL.md` and its `CLAUDE.md`, and create `Council Chamber/Logs/Autonomous Improvement Log.md` from the seed.

2. **Read the CLAUDE.md first, every run.** It is the operating mindset and it governs how the skill behaves. The steps tell the AI what to do; the CLAUDE.md tells it how to be.

3. **Adapt the starter menu to your ecosystem.** The shipped menu is generic. During onboarding, prune items your vault does not need and add hygiene types specific to your structure. Same for the research categories in the log frontmatter.

4. **Confirm your Permanent Floor.** Read the Constraints section and make sure the Floor matches your governance. The default Floor forbids deletion, canonical-governance moves, voice or outward-surface changes, main-branch pushes and self-modification. Implemented Pending Plan archival is explicitly above the Floor (operational lifecycle closure, reversible, no governance change), so the skill archives finished plans autonomously.

5. **Invoke it when you have capacity.** Say "run autonomous improvement" when you are heading out or closing a week with budget left. The skill runs, executes the safe work, and hands you a decision board. You answer it later.

6. **Optional: external-surface triage.** If your ecosystem maintains external repositories with issue tracking, enable the Step 0 extension described in the Menu section. It reads and routes external issues. It never pushes to a main branch.

## Prerequisites

v2.9.0 (Primer Surface and Single Lean Close) installed, since the closeout routes ripple-chasing to this skill. Foundation build complete. A git-tracked vault is recommended so Tier 1 edits are reversible via git, though the skill never deletes and every edit is additive or a clean in-place correction.

## Optional?

Yes. Skip it if you do not want autonomous improvement runs. The Session Closeout's batched-ripple-chasing note will simply have no receiver, which is harmless. Install it when you want to set the ecosystem improving while your attention is elsewhere.
