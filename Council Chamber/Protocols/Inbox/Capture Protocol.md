# Capture Protocol

Purpose: Define the operating rules for `Capture.md`, a root-level, low-friction capture surface for short-lived operational actions accessible from any device.

## Where Capture Sits

Capture is the first move of Tiago Forte's CODE: Capture, Organize, Distill, Express. This surface is the Capture move. Items leave it through the routes below, which is the Organize move. Distill and Express happen downstream, in the containers the items reach.

Earlier versions of this template called this surface the To-Do Dock. The job is the same. The name now says which move of the loop it is.

## Whose Surface This Is

**Capture is the Sovereign's own space. Your AI never adds to it.** These lines are your notes, your ideas and your to-dos that have no home yet. You act on them on your own clock.

Two things follow. Your AI reads Capture freely, and the reading is worth doing, because these lines carry context that exists nowhere else in the vault. And your AI never treats a Capture line as tracked state: nothing here is swept, chased, reconciled or reported as an open loop.

The one exception is your own words arriving through a pipe. A voice note you dictated as a to-do may land here after it is classified, because that is your capture reaching your surface. An item your AI thought of never becomes a Capture line. It is a dated reminder, it is raised with you live in the session, or it is nothing.

## What Belongs Here
- Items actionable within minutes, later today or within the next few days.
- Ideas and notes you are holding that have no home yet. These earn their place by carrying context, not by being near-term.
- One line per entry preferred.
- Not for archival, not for strategy, for action.

## Operating Rules
- Capture here when the item is a near-term action prompt that does not need archival retention.
- Complete, delete or promote entries during review. Do not preserve completed items.
- If an item grows in strategic weight, route it out using `[[Council Chamber/Protocols/Inbox/Capture Classify Route Protocol]]`.
- Mobile or voice captures routed to Capture should be classified in `Inbox/` first, then routed here when they match the to-do criteria.

## Promotion Rules
- Promote to InboxItem if the item is unclear, mixed, reference-heavy, needs analysis before action or shows expansion potential and wants deeper incubation.

## Review Prompt
- What can be done now?
- What should be deleted?
- What has outgrown Capture and should be promoted?

## What Capture Is Not
- Not an archive. Completed entries are deleted, not preserved.
- Not a project tracker. If it needs a plan, it needs a PendingPlan.
- Not an incubator. Growth energy routes to `Inbox/`.
