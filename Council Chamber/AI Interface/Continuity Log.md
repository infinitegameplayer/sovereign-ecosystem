# Continuity Log

Purpose: Track lightweight process improvements and continuity notes without creating heavy artifacts.

## Active
- Date: 2026-03-08
  Note: Continuity Log initialized as the lightweight surface for small process improvements and session-to-session learnings.

- Date: 2026-03-24
  Note: Capture corrections at the moment they occur — not only at session closeout. If the Sovereign corrects a behavior during a session, log it in the Continuity Log immediately before continuing the task. Waiting until closeout risks losing the correction if the session ends without a formal close. The same principle applies at the skill level: the Refinements section in each SKILL.md accumulates organically — never populated in bulk retroactively.

## Usage Rule
- Add short operational notes that improve future sessions.
- Keep major governance decisions in the Change Log.
- Archive stale items when they no longer improve future work.
