# Watch Register

One home for every standing watch, tripwire and confirm-flag in the ecosystem. Watches tend to live scattered in prose: revisit when volume rises, tripwire on validation, confirm before Friday. Scattered that way, they evaporate the way any unowned note evaporates. This register holds them so the weekly review can read them in one pass instead of hunting for them across dashboards.

**What belongs here.** A standing point of future attention that lives in prose: a tripwire that fires on a condition, a confirm-flag recorded pending confirmation, a "revisit when X" note, a "hold for a second occurrence" pattern watch, a "read at next Y" recurring attention item.

**What does not.** A task with a due date. Those live on the [[To-Do Dock]] and in the deadline scan. A standing sensing practice with no fireable condition (the North Star Drift Check) stays in its own ritual.

**How it is read.** At the weekly End-of-Week review. The review scans the register, checks each wake condition against the week and reconciles.

**How a watch leaves.** A watch leaves the register only three ways: it fires (the condition met, action taken or routed), it retires (no longer relevant), or it is handed an owner and a home that carries it (a plan, a skill step, a dashboard line). Nothing else removes a row. Log the departure in the Change Log below.

**It witnesses. It never nags.** Invitation over force. A watch notices. It does not whip.
