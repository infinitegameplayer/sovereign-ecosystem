# Primer

This is your forward handoff. The first thing your AI reads at the start of every session, ahead of anything else. It carries the rolling horizon: what is most alive, what is in motion, what is committed for the near term, what is parked, and how to open the next session.

Sovereign Command is the quick glance of what is most alive. The Primer is the fuller picture behind that glance. The git commit body is the backward record of what each session did. Three surfaces, three jobs. The Session Closeout refreshes this Primer every close, so it always reflects the freshly-settled state.

Right now it holds your onboarding orientation. As you operate, the Closeout reshapes it into your live horizon.

## Most Alive Next Move

Build the foundation. You just initialized your Sovereign Ecosystem. The most alive move is to get oriented and stand the structure up through real use, not to admire it. The Getting Started folder is your on-ramp.

## In Execution

The Getting Started build path. Sessions 0 through 9 take you from prerequisites to a live operating rhythm, with Optional Paths to branch into once the foundation holds. The sequence is a suggested order, not a march. Take the sessions in the order that serves you, skip what does not fit yet, and return to anything later. The structure is meant to be edited as you go.

## Active Commitments

Your first move: open the Getting Started folder and start with the Quick Start Guide, or Session 0 Prerequisites if you want the full ground-up path. From there, Session 1 initializes the vault and the adapter, and each session builds on a live foundation rather than a planned one.

## Parked

Optional Paths (AI Interface personalization, a Creator or Venture HQ surface, a lightweight life-operating surface) wait here until the foundation is live. Pick them up when the core rhythm is running and one of them earns its place.
